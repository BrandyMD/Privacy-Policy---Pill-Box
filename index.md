# Privacy Policy for Pill Box

**Last Updated:** September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Pill Box** ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Brandy Disher, handles information when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Core App Functionality & Local Data Storage

Pill Box is designed to prioritize user privacy and simplicity:

* **Routine & Compartment Status:** Your medication reminder schedules, refill states, and daily pill compartment toggles are stored locally on your device storage.
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide personal credentials to use the App.
* **No Medical Records:** The App does not collect, access, or transmit your sensitive health records, prescriptions, or medical history.

---

## 2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

While the App does not directly collect or store your personal identity or health details, we integrate trusted third-party SDKs to support app performance analysis and serve advertisements.

### A. Google Analytics for Firebase
We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App and improve stability. Firebase automatically processes non-personally identifiable diagnostic and usage telemetry, including:
* Device state, device model, and operating system version.
* App launch frequency, session duration, and screen engagement.
* Anonymous app instance identifiers.

### B. Google AdMob
We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob may automatically collect and use device data to deliver ads, including:
* Android Advertising ID (AAID).
* IP address (used for coarse geographic location and fraud prevention).
* Ad interaction events (views, taps, and impressions).

For more details on how Google processes this data, review:
* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps that Use Our Services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

---

## 3. How We Use Collected Data

Any data gathered through third-party services is used exclusively for:

* Monitoring application performance, detecting crashes, and ensuring app stability.
* Internal analytics to understand feature usage and improve user experience.
* Serving contextual or personalized advertisements in accordance with Google policies.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties.

---

## 4. Opting Out of Targeted Advertising & Tracking

You have full control over advertising identifiers on your Android device:

* **Reset or Delete Advertising ID:** Navigate to **Settings > Google > Ads** (or **Settings > Privacy > Ads**) on your Android device to reset your advertising ID or delete it entirely.
* **App Permissions:** You can adjust or revoke app permissions (such as notifications) anytime in your device's application settings.

---

## 5. Data Security

We value your trust. Because your routine data remains strictly on your device, its physical security depends on your device security settings. Third-party data transmissions via Google services are encrypted in transit using standard security protocols (HTTPS/TLS).

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our third-party services, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

---

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. You are advised to review this page periodically for updates.
